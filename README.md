# `@bethinkpl/reorder`
Customized fork of [@reorder/reorderjs](https://github.com/reorder-js/reorder).
## Requirements / breaking changes
- Medusa `>= 2.17.2` is required (the `@medusajs/*` peer floor is `^2.17.2`). On older versions a payment authorisation
  that is still pending throws instead of returning `null`, so the plugin records it as a failed charge and starts
  dunning for a payment that may still succeed
- the plugin's cancellation workflows (`cancelSubscriptionWorkflow`, `finalizeCancellationWorkflow`,
  `cancelSubscriptionByCustomerWorkflow`), `pauseSubscriptionWorkflow`, `resumeSubscriptionWorkflow` and
  `applyRetentionOfferWorkflow` take the `renewal:subscription:<subscription_id>` lock and the `dunning:<case_id>` lock
  of every active dunning case themselves. A host app must not hold either lock around them: the workflow then waits
  for it and fails with a lock conflict (for example a host wrapper that took `renewal:subscription:<id>` around
  `cancelSubscriptionWorkflow` now times out)
- `runDunningRetryWorkflow` can resolve `outcome: "awaiting_payment"`; code that switches over its outcomes has to
  handle it
- the down-migrations map the new status back lossily: an `awaiting_payment` dunning case becomes `retrying` and an
  `awaiting_payment` renewal cycle becomes `processing` (its `settlement_inputs` are dropped)
- the admin cancel and the finalised cancellation now close the subscription's active dunning cases; a host app that closed them
  itself after calling these workflows no longer needs to
- the awaiting-payment sweeps (`jobs:reconcile-awaiting-renewal-cycles`, `jobs:reconcile-paid-dunning-cases`), the
  renewal scheduler (`jobs:renewal-cycles`) and the subscription and dunning-case locks (`renewal:subscription:<id>`,
  `dunning:<case_id>`, taken by renewal runs, dunning retries, the settlement of a confirmed charge, the cancellation,
  pause and resume workflows and the resolve routes) are held with `locking.acquire` and an explicit lifetime, and rely
  on the locking provider expiring a lock whose holder died. The Redis (`@medusajs/locking-redis`) and in-memory
  providers do. `@medusajs/locking-postgres` (checked on 2.22.0) does not: its `acquire` never hands another owner a
  row of its `locking` table, expired or not, so a process that crashes or is killed while holding one of these locks
  leaves that key blocked (a sweep that never runs again, a subscription that can no longer renew, be cancelled or
  paused, a dunning case that is never retried) until the row is deleted, e.g.
  `DELETE FROM locking WHERE expiration < NOW()` after a crash. Locks taken with `locking.execute` (transaction-scoped
  advisory locks) are released with their connection and are not affected

## Changes
### New features
- added day granularity for frequency intervals
- support for payment providers that confirm a charge later: when `authorizePaymentSession` returns `null`, the renewal cycle or dunning case waits as `awaiting_payment` until `payment.captured` settles it or a `failed`/`canceled` `payment.webhook_received` fails it (a renewal then enters dunning, a dunning retry is rescheduled); requires `medusa db:migrate`. The storefront sees a dunning retry whose charge awaits the provider as a retry in progress (`payment_recovery.state: "retrying"`, `retry_blocked_reason: "retry_in_progress"`); only the admin API reports it as `payment_in_progress`
- `POST /admin/renewals/:id/resolve-awaiting-payment` settles or fails a renewal cycle whose payment result never arrived; a charge waiting longer than `RENEWAL_AWAITING_PAYMENT_ALERT_HOURS` (default `96`) raises an alertable log. An unpaid charge can be resolved as failed (which starts dunning) only once it has waited longer than `RENEWAL_AWAITING_PAYMENT_RESOLVE_MIN_HOURS` (default `96`, set independently of the alert so that lowering the alert never lets an admin fail a charge the provider may still settle; keep it above the slowest settlement of your providers, e.g. ~73 h for BLIK via Przelewy24)
- `POST /admin/dunning/:id/resolve-awaiting-payment` does the same for a dunning case awaiting its retry's payment: recovers it when the renewal order is paid, or fails the retry as a failure webhook would once it has waited longer than `RENEWAL_AWAITING_PAYMENT_RESOLVE_MIN_HOURS`; marking such a case recovered or unrecovered is refused while its charge is in flight
- a `failed`/`canceled` payment webhook that arrives while another run keeps the renewal (`renewal:subscription:<id>`) or dunning (`dunning:<id>`) lock for more than 5 seconds is recorded on the attempt it names (`reported_payment_failure`; requires `medusa db:migrate`) with a non-alertable log, and the delivery succeeds instead of being retried by the event bus; the `reconcile-awaiting-renewal-cycles` and `reconcile-paid-dunning-cases` jobs (every 30 minutes) apply it under the lock as the webhook would have, unless the renewal order has been paid in the meantime
- a dunning case its retry left `retrying` (its run could not mark it as awaiting the charge, or stopped before writing an outcome) is picked up by `reconcile-paid-dunning-cases` once the retry is 10 minutes old: with a submitted charge (the attempt is still `processing` and has its payment session) it moves to `awaiting_payment`, so the charge's capture or failure, the overdue alert and the resolve route apply to it; otherwise it moves to `awaiting_manual_resolution` with `park_reason: "indeterminate_provider_response"`, an alertable log and `subscription.dunning_parked`, since nothing tells whether that retry charged the card
- `processRenewalCycleWorkflow` now resolves `{ outcome, renewal_cycle, subscription_id, attempt_id, generated_order_id }` for every run that does not throw, where `outcome` (`RenewalRunOutcome`, exported from `@bethinkpl/reorder/workflows`) is `succeeded`, `awaiting_payment`, `park_failed` (charge submitted, cycle still `processing`, `renewal_cycle: null`) or `failed`; it used to resolve only the settled cycle's `{ renewal_cycle, ... }`. The scheduler job and the admin force action report this outcome instead of re-reading the cycle after the lock is released (a `park_failed` run counts as awaiting in the job)
- a renewal cycle left `processing` by a run that submitted its charge but could not park it is settled by `reconcile-awaiting-renewal-cycles` once its newest attempt is 10 minutes old and its renewal order is paid: a capture that meets another holder of the renewal lock (logged, non-alertable, as deferred to the sweep) or never arrives is not delivered again
### Improvements / Fixes
- prevents executing `POST /store/carts/{cartId}/complete` for carts containing subscription items
- behaviour of `POST /store/carts/{cartId}/subscribe` matches it's non-subscription counterpart more closely (dropped `subscription` field, supports query params and uses same defaults)
- shipping address is optional when item(s) in subscription cart don't require shipping (i.e.: `item.requires_shipping = false`)
- exposed `setPaymentSessionData` hook in `processRenewalCycleWorkflow` for provider-agnostic payment handling during renewals
- exposed the same `setPaymentSessionData` hook in `runDunningRetryWorkflow` (input: `{ subscription: { id, payment_context } }`) so dunning retries use the host app's payment session payload, falling back to the stock Stripe payload when no handler is registered
- dunning now emits five lifecycle events (names exported as `DunningEvents` from `@bethinkpl/reorder/modules/dunning`) for the host app to notify customers on: `subscription.dunning_started` (`{ subscription_id, dunning_case_id, renewal_cycle_id, attempt_count, next_retry_at }`, fired only when a case is created — a repeated failure on the same renewal cycle re-enters the existing case and stays silent), `subscription.dunning_attempt_failed` (`{ subscription_id, dunning_case_id, attempt_no, error_code, next_retry_at }`, fired when a counted retry failed and another one is scheduled; no-budget reschedules stay silent - a tick that charged nothing and handed its attempt slot back never tells the customer their payment failed), `subscription.payment_failed` (`{ subscription_id, dunning_case_id, recovery_reason }`, fired only by the run that actually moves the subscription to `payment_failed`, from either the exhausted retry loop or the admin "mark unrecovered" action — a later settlement of an already-`payment_failed` subscription stays silent), `subscription.dunning_parked` (`{ subscription_id, dunning_case_id, attempt_no, park_reason, error_code }`, fired when a retry is handed to an operator instead of being settled or rescheduled; `park_reason` is one of `setup_failure`, `requires_action`, `customer_payment_stalled`, `unreached_provider` or `indeterminate_provider_response`) and `subscription.dunning_recovered` (`{ subscription_id, dunning_case_id, renewal_order_id, recovery_reason }`, fired when a case closes as recovered — `recovery_reason` is `payment_recovered` when a scheduled retry collected it and `customer_payment` when the customer paid the renewal order themselves through the host app, which also settles the renewal cycle, advances the billing dates and hands the subscription back to `active`). Delivery is at-most-once and immediate: each event is dispatched as soon as its emitting step runs, once the module writes that triggered it have committed, so a later step failing in the same workflow (e.g. the lock release) cannot retract an event that already went out, and a rejecting bus is never retried. Emitting never fails the workflow either — an event bus that rejects is logged as an `alertable` dunning error and the case keeps its state
- a captured payment this plugin will not apply is reported as `subscription.late_payment_captured` (name exported as `SubscriptionEvents.LATE_PAYMENT_CAPTURED` from `@bethinkpl/reorder/modules/subscription`, payload `{ subscription_id, payment_id, order_id, payment_collection_id, renewal_cycle_id?, dunning_case_id?, dunning_attempt_id?, reason, refund_amount, currency_code }`) so the host app can refund it; the plugin never refunds itself. `refund_amount` is in the payment's currency, in the units Medusa stores amounts in: the payment's captured amount less its refunds, except for `order_overpaid`, where it is only the order's surplus not already claimed by another reported capture (never more than the payment's unrefunded amount). It fires for a capture of a session the plugin created for a renewal or a dunning retry when the subscription is cancelled or otherwise terminal (`reason: subscription_terminal`; a `payment_failed` subscription whose dunning case names the churn reversal is the exception, since reversing the churn keeps the money), or when the renewal order ends up over-paid (`cycle_closed` / `case_closed` when the charge's cycle or case had already closed, `order_overpaid` otherwise; an over-payment already reported for another capture and not yet refunded is not reported again). At most once per payment: the attempt that submitted the charge records the payment before the event is dispatched, a redelivered capture does nothing, and a rejecting bus is logged as alertable and never retried. Each emission also writes an alertable log line
- renewals and dunning retries capture through the payment module directly, which emits nothing: `payment.captured` (and any host invoicing, tax or email hanging off it) arrives only when the provider's own webhook for that charge is delivered and the host's provider maps it to a successful action. With a missing or mis-signed webhook endpoint the subscription still renews and a dunning case still reads `recovered`, and nothing errors
- a scheduled dunning retry now steps aside instead of destroying a payment the customer is in the middle of: before it touches the renewal order it reads every payment session on every payment collection of that order, and when one is still live (status `pending`, `pending_authorization` or `requires_more`, created less than 60 minutes ago and not past its own `data.expiresAt`) or a sibling collection holds somebody else's authorization (`authorized` or `partially_authorized`), the tick charges nothing - it aborts its attempt row, hands the attempt slot back to the budget and reschedules itself 60 minutes later without notifying the customer, never touching the case setup-failure streak and only parking the case (`park_reason: "customer_payment_stalled"`) once 24 ticks in a row have stepped aside (an admin `retry now` is skipped the same way; the skips are counted in `metadata.session_conflict_count`, which is informational and cleared as soon as a retry reaches the provider). Sessions a retry creates mark themselves with `context: { dunning_case_id, dunning_attempt_id }` and sessions a renewal run creates with `context: { renewal_cycle_id }`, so neither ever reads the other's session - or its own leftovers, including an authorization a failed capture left behind - as a customer's. A host app that creates its own payment sessions on a renewal order is protected as long as it sets neither marker on them, and it can mark a session `context: { initiated_by: "customer" }` to be treated as the customer's whatever else it carries
- the daily renewal job applies the same guard before it reuses an order a previous run already created: if the customer is mid-checkout on it, the run is skipped before it records anything - no attempt row, no new failure, no dunning case - so the cycle keeps the `failed` status it already had and the next window picks it up again. The job counts the skip as `blocked` rather than `failed` (`failure_kind: "customer_payment_in_progress"`, never alertable)
- completing subscription cart no longer requires payment method to be already saved at that point of time, instead it's info is backfilled after payment is captured
- renewal orders recompute the subscription plan discount instead of billing full catalog price (frozen `pricing_snapshot` first, live plan config on plan change), with a combined-discount clamp applied on both checkout and renewals
- exposed `resolveRenewalAdjustments` hook in `processRenewalCycleWorkflow` so the host app can add its own (code-less) discounts to renewal orders, and `subscriptionCreated` hook in `createSubscriptionFromCartWorkflow` for reacting to genuine subscription creation
- `source_snapshot` now truthfully captures the initial order's adjustments and tax lines (audit-only — renewals deliberately no longer replay them)
- renewal cycles stranded in PROCESSING by an unhandled failure between steps are now marked FAILED via compensation, keeping them retryable; a run that submitted its charge but could not park the cycle (`park_failed`) is the exception and stays PROCESSING until its capture or the awaiting-payment sweep settles it
- a renewal that succeeds after a failed attempt (dunning retry, the customer paying the renewal order, a re-run of a failed cycle) now counts the next billing period from the day it was paid instead of from the original due date, and so does a first attempt that succeeds so late that its next date has already passed; on-time renewals keep their original schedule
- a failed renewal cycle is no longer re-run daily while its subscription has an active dunning case; dunning alone retries the charge, and it does not call `applyRenewalCredits`, so credits gained while the case is open are not applied unless the host app adds them to the renewal order itself (see `src/workflows/README.md`)
- cancellation workflows take the `renewal:subscription:<id>` lock themselves (a host app must not hold it when calling them), end a renewal cycle that is awaiting its payment and close every active dunning case, including one a retry run left `retrying` once its latest attempt is 10 minutes old (a younger one may still be waiting for the provider, so the cancellation is refused with a `409` and changes nothing) (the customer's cancellation and the finalised cancellation with `recovery_reason: "subscription_cancelled_by_customer"`, the admin cancel route with `subscription_not_retryable`); the charge such a cycle or case submitted stays tracked: its attempt is left `processing`, a late failure closes it as failed with no dunning and no event, and a late capture closes it as `succeeded` with error code `captured_after_close` and is reported as `subscription.late_payment_captured`. The awaiting-payment sweeps end an awaiting cycle of an already cancelled or `payment_failed` subscription, and close an active dunning case of a cancelled one, deliver again a lost capture of such an attempt, and fail one with no result after `RENEWAL_AWAITING_PAYMENT_RESOLVE_MIN_HOURS` (error code `no_payment_signal`). A cancellation that fails part way leaves nothing changed, and the cancel routes answer a conflict with a readable message
- renewal payment sessions carry `context.renewal_attempt_id` next to `renewal_cycle_id`
- a dunning recovery and a renewal settlement never apply a payment to a cancelled subscription: such a capture is reconciled as a late payment and reported once for a refund. A renewal run whose charge went through only after the subscription was cancelled (its lock lapsed) ends its cycle as `failed` the way the cancellation ends an awaiting one, with the attempt kept on its payment session, resolves `outcome: "failed"` instead of `succeeded`, and reconciles the capture as a late payment. This includes a retry whose charge the provider took only after its run's case lock had lapsed: the run reads the case and the subscription again before closing the case as recovered, and when a cancellation closed it, or the paid-case sweep moved it on, in the meantime it writes nothing, leaves its attempt to whoever owns the case now and reconciles the capture as a late payment
- a payment failure that arrives after the renewal order was paid another way (for example by the customer through the host app) no longer fails the renewal or reschedules the dunning case; the awaiting-payment sweeps settle or recover it from the paid order
- a capture on a `payment_failed` subscription is reported for a refund when its churn can't be reversed because the customer's own cancellation stands, and is left for the dunning sweep to deliver again when recovering its case failed
- a renewal cycle closed as superseded still gets its next cycle scheduled (the scheduling is idempotent), so a settlement that moved the billing dates but could not close the cycle no longer stops the subscription from renewing
- the awaiting-payment sweeps raise an alertable log (`renewal.awaiting_payment_session_missing`, `dunning.awaiting_payment_session_missing`) on every pass for a charge whose payment session no longer exists with nothing captured on it, e.g. after the customer opened a new payment session on the renewal order; nothing is failed automatically
- resolving an awaiting charge as failed through an admin route keeps the admin's `reason` on the attempt (`metadata.admin_resolution`) and in the logs; the storefront only sees a fixed message
- the storefront cancellation route answers a workflow refusal with generic texts and never with the workflow's own message
- the paid-case sweep pages through every idle dunning case instead of the 200 least recently updated, so cases parked for manual resolution no longer hide the rest
- the awaiting-payment sweeps, the resolve routes and the settlement of a confirmed charge hold their locks with an explicit lifetime (on Medusa 2.17.2 with Redis `locking.execute` uses its wait as the lock's lifetime); each sweep's job lock lasts at most 25 minutes with a locking provider that expires locks (see the requirements above for `@medusajs/locking-postgres`)
- pausing (admin, storefront, or a retention `pause_offer`) and resuming a subscription are refused with a `409` while a renewal charge or a dunning retry charge waits for the payment provider's answer (the storefront answer carries `code: "renewal_payment_in_progress"`), so a late settlement can no longer move the billing dates of a paused subscription or overlap a charge with the cycle a resume creates. `pauseSubscriptionWorkflow`, `resumeSubscriptionWorkflow` and `applyRetentionOfferWorkflow` take the `renewal:subscription:<id>` lock themselves, and the `dunning:<case_id>` lock of every active dunning case until their change is written, so a dunning retry can't charge in between (a host app must hold neither when calling them)
- a renewal settled after its run (a charge the provider confirms later, a dunning recovery) clears `pending_update_data` only while it is still the plan change the cycle applied, so a plan change scheduled in the meantime stays pending; a cycle whose charge awaited the provider, or that dunning recovered, also keeps a delivery skip (`skip_next_cycle`) requested in the meantime
- workflow compensations no longer restore `payment_context`, so a payment method saved in the meantime is kept
- the admin dunning, renewal, cancellation and subscription-settings routes answer a workflow's refusal with its own message and the status of its `MedusaError` type (`not_found` 404, `invalid_data` 400, `conflict`/`not_allowed` 409, other types 500), and a lock another run holds with a readable 409; before, the dunning routes hid every workflow message behind "Unexpected dunning admin error", the renewal and cancellation routes answered invalid input with a 409 and the settings route could answer it with a 500
- the storefront retry route answers a retry the workflow refuses after the eligibility check passed (another run holds the case, or its charge started meanwhile) with the same `409` and `blocked_reason` as that check, in the storefront's vocabulary; the storefront cancellation route answers every busy-lock reply of the locking providers with its "already being updated" `409` instead of a `500`
